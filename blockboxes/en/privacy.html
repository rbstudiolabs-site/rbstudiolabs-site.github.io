Block Boxes ("the game") is developed by RB Studio Labs (Royal Babayev, Azerbaijan). This policy explains what information is used when you play Block Boxes and how you can control it.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account. You can play without signing in.
  • Your progress (scores, levels, gold and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information and the advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • With your consent, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are hard). No name or email is collected.
  • The game can show notifications: news from us and a daily reminder. You can turn them off at any time.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, Adventure progress, gold, purchases made in the game (such as "Remove ads"), the chosen language and the sound and vibration settings in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads: a banner at the bottom of the game screen, full-screen ads between games and levels, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue a game or to earn gold). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads can be personalized. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy options" in Settings. Elsewhere, you can limit personalized ads in your device settings.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve the game, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It records how the game is used, for example: game sessions and their length, levels started, completed or failed, Classic scores, ads watched in the game, gold used and purchases made. With these events Google also processes an app instance ID, device information (model, operating system, language, app version) and the approximate country derived from your IP address. The advertising ID is not collected by Analytics and the data is not used for ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics data is collected only after you give consent in the privacy message shown by the game (the same choice as for ads, which you can change with "Privacy options" in Settings).

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the game asks for permission to show notifications when it is opened for the first time. If you allow it, you may receive news about the game sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and a reminder created on your device itself if you have not played for a day. To deliver messages, Firebase uses a device token that does not identify you personally. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, either completely or separately for "News" and "Reminders".

In-app purchases

Purchases ("Remove ads" and gold packs) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver the item and restore it later. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

Internet connection check

When the game starts, it sends a simple request to a Google server (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com) to find out whether the device is online. The request contains no personal information beyond what any internet connection reveals, such as your IP address. On some devices the game uses a similar Huawei server (connectivitycheck.platform.hicloud.com) instead.

Children

Block Boxes is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the services described above (Google AdMob, Google Play and the App Store), the game does not share data with anyone. Analytics data and notification tokens are processed by Google (Firebase) on our behalf as a service provider.

Data retention and your rights

We do not keep your data on our own servers. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to its processing. Contact us and we will help with any request. Analytics data is kept by Google for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

Security

Data on your device is protected by the standard security of your device, and the services described above transmit data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
